✗

No dice! Unlike tabletop RPGs like D&D, success or failure isn't determined by chance or a roll.

✓

Collaborative, in person storytelling

Generally fantasy focused but there are many genres!

Combat Safety

Fantasy Alive is a "boffer LARP" which means combat is simulated with padded foam weapons. Characters have Life Points and Armour Points (if they're wearing armour). Based on the weapon or skill used, a number is called out and that many points are deducted from the target. Other effects like stuns are called the same way.

Magic works much the same, delivered either through touch, a foam "fist boffer" or a thrown packet. The caster calls out the spell effect and then does the action. If you're ever unsure about what a spell does, it's okay to ask!

Fantasy Alive encourages dramatic combat! Big actions and reactions are encouraged, from yelps of surprise to groans of mock pain. For safety reasons, the head and groin are always off-limits for attacks.

"ZEBRA!"

Accidents happen, especially outdoors. If someone needs combat to stop immediately (often because of a real injury), they shout "Zebra!" Since zebras don't exist anywhere in the game world, it can't be mistaken for anything in game. This makes it the unmistakable signal to pause until it's safe to continue with the phrase: "Lay on."

FAQ

Can I participate if I have reduced mobility or other barriers?

Absolutely! In fact, Fantasy Alive has a specific non-combat system for those that want to opt out of physical combat entirely, regardless of their disability or if they simply don't want to participate in that aspect and our management is also happy to find ways to get everyone involved!

How do I get props/costumes/weapons/etc?

Our aesthetic is medieval fantasy but it's not strictly enforced nor do we enforce any kind of "gender norm". There are many players who are happy to lend equipment if you're in need but if you're looking at your own kit, thrifting is a great way to get started! We just ask you avoid jeans and logos on your clothes! It's best to start out simple and build your look over time!
