Site Amenities

This site has several amenities intended to make your LARP adventure more convenient and comfortable.

On-site parking
Hot and cold running water
An indoor bathroom
Outhouse facilities
Showers
A wood-burning stove inside the large 'tavern' building
A fire pit outside
Outdoor picnic table seating
Cabins for indoor sleeping (limited availability, contact Logistics to request a space)

We also provide a healthy and nutritious meal plan for events. Please purchase it accordingly if you're interested. Your characters may be roughing it, but you can take the time to enjoy your adventure with the help of our site amenities.

Don't be afraid to live twice, and to live both times in a clean and comfortable environment.
