Manage Downtime

Staff access required

This page is only available to Logistics staff.

Adjust for a Character

Adds or removes downtime Hours for one character at the selected event -- e.g. compensating a character who missed downtime due to a plot event. Use a negative number to remove hours.

Adjust for the Whole Player Base

Adds or removes downtime Hours for every registered character at the selected event -- e.g. docking hours site-wide for a shortened event.
