The Niagara Region's largest LARP!

Based in South Western Ontario, Fantasy Alive has been running for over 20 years! Set in the world of Ariel, experience exploration, excitement, and the stories of everyday people who become heroes!

With kingdoms recently shaken by war and trying to rebuild a fragile peace, bandits, undead, demons, and monsters beset small towns far from the cities of power. The gods work on their own machinations, assisting both heroes and villains in plans no mortal can fully grasp.

In this game, you'll face puzzles, traps, and great battles. You'll meet merchants, priests, thieves, and wizards, and more. Between games, our downtime system lets your character grow their skills, craft, research, and travel.

The world is waiting for its next great tale of heroism.
